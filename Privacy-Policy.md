<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>

<p><strong>ForgetMeNot: Wallet</strong><br>
Last updated: February 2026</p>

<hr>

<h2>Our Commitment</h2>

<p>
ForgetMeNot is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app helps you keep track
of value you already own — such as gift cards, perks, points, and stored balances —
without requiring an account or sending your data to our servers.
</p>

<p>
ForgetMeNot does not provide payment processing, financial aggregation, budgeting,
or financial advisory services.
</p>

<p><strong>The short version:</strong> We do not collect or store your wallet data on our servers.
The information you enter into ForgetMeNot is stored locally on your device.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Information We Collect</h2>

<p>
The current version of ForgetMeNot does not collect personally identifiable information
(such as your name, email address, or physical address) and does not transmit your wallet
contents (value items, notes, attachments, or balances) to our servers.
</p>

<p>
We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries inside
the app.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Information You Store in the App</h2>

<p>
ForgetMeNot allows you to store information such as:
</p>

<ul>
<li>Gift cards and stored value balances</li>
<li>Store returns, refunds, and promotional value</li>
<li>Loyalty points or reward balances</li>
<li>Recurring value items such as credit card perks</li>
<li>Optional notes about where or how you received value</li>
<li>Optional attachments (photos or screenshots)</li>
</ul>

<p>
This information is stored locally on your device using the app’s private storage.
We do not have access to this data and do not sync it to our servers.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Attachments (Photos and Screenshots)</h2>

<p>
You can optionally attach photos or screenshots (for example, a picture of a gift card or
a receipt) to a value item. These images are stored locally on your device within the app’s
sandboxed storage.
</p>

<p>
We do not upload these attachments to our servers and do not use them for analytics,
training, or advertising.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Local Notifications</h2>

<p>
ForgetMeNot uses <strong>local notifications</strong> to remind you about upcoming expirations
and recurring value items such as credit card perks.
</p>

<p>
These notifications are scheduled on your device and based only on the data you enter.
Scheduling information is stored locally and is not sent to our servers.
</p>

<p>
You can control notification permissions at any time in your device settings.
If you revoke notification permissions, the app will stop showing reminders.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Camera and Photo Library Access</h2>

<p>
ForgetMeNot may request access to:
</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Camera</strong> – to take photos of cards, receipts, or proof of value.</li>
<li><strong>Photo Library</strong> – to attach existing screenshots or images.</li>
</ul>

<p>
The app may also use the camera for optional on-device scanning to read card numbers or
PINs. All scanning and processing happen locally on your device. No images or extracted
data are uploaded to our servers.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>On-Device Processing</h2>

<p>
Some features — such as optional card scanning — use on-device processing to help extract
information directly from images. This processing happens entirely on your device and
does not send data externally.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>How We Use Your Data</h2>

<p>
Within the app, your data is used solely to:
</p>

<ul>
<li>Display your value items in the wallet interface.</li>
<li>Calculate totals such as active value and expiring items.</li>
<li>Determine when to schedule local reminders.</li>
<li>Show attachments alongside the relevant value item.</li>
</ul>

<p>
We do not use your data for profiling, advertising, behavioral tracking,
or cross-site tracking.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Network Connections and Third-Party Services</h2>

<p>
ForgetMeNot does not send your wallet contents (value balances, notes, attachments,
or card numbers) to our servers or to third-party analytics providers.
</p>

<p>
However, the app relies on platform services to function, for example:
</p>

<ul>
<li>Apple App Store services for downloads and updates.</li>
<li>Operating system services that deliver local notifications.</li>
<li>External links you choose to open (such as this policy).</li>
</ul>

<p>
These providers may collect technical information according to their own policies.
We do not receive or store that information.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Children’s Privacy</h2>

<p>
ForgetMeNot is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children. Because wallet data stays on your device, we do not have access
to individual usage data.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Data Retention and Deletion</h2>

<p>
All wallet data is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain server copies.
</p>

<ul>
<li>If you delete a value item or attachment, it is removed from your device.</li>
<li>If you uninstall the app, the operating system deletes all local data.</li>
</ul>

<p>
Because we do not store your data, we cannot restore or recover lost information.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Changes to This Policy</h2>

<p>
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features, legal requirements,
or improvements to the app. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date.
The latest version will always be available in the app’s Settings screen.
</p>
